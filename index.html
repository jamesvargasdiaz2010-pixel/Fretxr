🎸 FretXR

Electric guitar · mixed reality note highway

  1. Have your amp audible (the headset mic listens for notes).
  2. Hold guitar in playing position. Touch the front tip of your controller to each point and pull the trigger:
    ① nut at the low E string → ② nut at the high E string → ③ 12th fret, middle of the neck.
  3. Set the controller down, then pinch (or press trigger) to start. Grip button = re-align anytime.
